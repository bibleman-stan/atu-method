Claims this repo made and later withdrew. **Cross-corpus and methodology retractions belong here**; corpus-specific ones stay in the reader repo that made the claim.

Created 2026-08-11. The hub had no retraction log while three reader repos did — `readers-bofm` 16 entries, `readers-gnt` 10, `readers-tanakh` 5 — which meant the repo that authors the methodology was the one repo not recording when its methodology claims failed.

## What belongs here

A retraction, not a correction. The distinction that matters: a **correction** fixes a mistake in doing the work and belongs in [[4-process/lessons.md]]; a **retraction** withdraws a claim that was published, believed, and acted on. If nothing downstream ever depended on it, it is a correction.

## Format

Each entry: what was claimed, where it was published, what falsified it, what replaced it, and what downstream work rested on it while it stood.

## The loop this feeds, and its current state

Retractions are supposed to accumulate until a *pattern* earns a standing rule — the same shape as [[4-process/lessons.md]] → the standing defaults in [[CLAUDE.md]], which fired on 2026-08-09.

**That promotion has never fired for retractions.** As of 2026-08-11: **31 entries across three repos, zero promotions, corpus-wide.** `loop_health.py` reports it every session.

Two readings, and they need different responses:

1. **The loop is broken** — entries accumulate and nothing ever reviews them for a pattern.
2. **The threshold is honest** — no retraction has yet recurred often enough to earn a rule, and firing would manufacture doctrine from noise.

The count alone cannot distinguish these, and guessing would be worse than waiting. **Open for Stan.**
