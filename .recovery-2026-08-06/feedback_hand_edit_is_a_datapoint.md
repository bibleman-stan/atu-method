A hand-edit (Stan's or anyone's) **can and maybe should be a component** of the process — but it is **one data point, not a definitive weight, and never the ground truth.** Stan (2026-05-22): *"a human like myself might not understand the grammar / see the big picture / be fully aligned [consistent]."*

**Why this matters:** the entire point of the mechanical-first method is **consistency and scalability that transcends any single human's inconsistency.** A human editor (a) may not know the underlying grammar (e.g. miss that a fronted participle is attendant-circumstance, or that a relative is cataphoric), (b) may not see the systematic/big-picture pattern (edits ad-hoc, verse by verse), (c) drifts across time (criteria evolve — BoFM v2-mine was done under several evolving, inconsistent criteria). So the method's job is to be **more consistent than any human pass**, and a hand-edit carries **no special authority** over the method's own standard.

**How to use a hand-edit correctly — triangulate, don't defer:**
- It is **one signal among several**: canon-conformance (`run_all`), the bidirectional ATU test, cross-corpus convergence, discourse references (LDGNT/LDHB), AND the hand-edit. None is the oracle; the hand-edit is not weighted above the rest.
- A **hand-edit ⇄ method DIVERGENCE is a QUESTION, not a verdict.** Investigate *which is right* by the method's principles: does the divergence expose a real rule GAP (→ improve the rule; this is the refinement engine) or a human error/inconsistency (→ the method is right, the hand-edit is the noisy data point)? Resolve by the bidirectional test + canon, **not by the human's authority.**
- The hand-edit's genuine value is **surfacing candidate boundaries the mechanical pass missed** — a cheap source of test cases and gap-hypotheses. That is real and worth using. It just doesn't get to override.

**The anti-pattern (what I did, 2026-05-21/22):** I gave the hand-edit *definitive weight* — steering v2/v3 toward matching v2-mine's line counts (the 0.787 "vs your 122" framing, the v3 freehand merge toward 122). That substituted a noisy, drifted human data point for the method's own yardstick. Correct stance: the hand-edit is a column in the triangulation table, not the answer key. Ties to [[feedback-check-prior-corpora]] (the wheel + canon-conformance yardstick) and the convergence discipline.
