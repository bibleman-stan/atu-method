## How to apply

- When vault-hygiene opportunities surface (under-tagged notes, mis-classified zettels, folder drift, stub accumulation), **propose AND execute proactively** rather than asking Stan to decide every move.
- Reserve Stan's bandwidth for genuine judgment calls — naming, theoretical commitments, scholarly framing, scope-discipline, what-belongs-in-which-paper.
- The clearer the v8 rule (or other authoritative system doc), the more autonomously to apply it. The murkier the call, the more it warrants Stan's eye.
- Surface "I did X" via tight after-action summaries; don't pad with offers to undo. Don't ask permission for moves that follow durable system rules.
- Sample-first / manifest-then-execute protocols still apply for large irreversible moves. Speed *within* safety; not speed at any cost.
