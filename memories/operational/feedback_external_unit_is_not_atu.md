When mining colometry/discourse scholarship (Scheppers, Marschall, Korpel, et al.), **the unit they isolate is NOT identical to our ATU.** Their breaking criteria/patterns **may overlap our boundaries and be extremely useful — but they do not replace our method.** Stan (2026-05-26): "be careful that these scholars are proposing to isolate features not necessarily identifiable with our ATUs"; "they might overlap, and their breaking criteria/patterns might be extremely useful, but they do not replace our method." Hold this as a standing guardrail.

**The bidirectional ATU test is the sole arbiter.** External criteria are *feedstock* filtered through it; none overrides, defines, or substitutes for the method.

**Why:** each scholar's target object differs from the idea-unit, and the mismatch runs BOTH ways:
- **Scheppers' colon** = the prosodic *intonation/information unit* — **finer** than the ATU (>36% sub-clausal; a fronted NP is its own colon). His "fronting makes a colon" rule = our documented over-split failure mode.
- **Marschall's côlon** = a *rhetorical/sound-mapping* unit with syllable bounds (7–25, ≤35); her *comma* shorter, her *period* (2–4 côla) sometimes **coarser** than an ATU. Syllable thresholds are prosodic.
- **Korpel's colon** sits in a 6-level hierarchy (foot→colon→line→strophe→canticle→macro), accent/MS-anchored — line/strophe are supra-ATU; poetic cola can be sub-ATU.

**How to apply:**
1. **Architecture + individual features are triangulation CANDIDATES, never unit-identity.** Any criterion (γάρ-boundary, full-GU condition, parallelism constraint, RC-split, ellipsis→bind) must pass OUR bidirectional test (forward grammatical closure + backward referential self-containment) on OUR unit before entering a binding-rule catalog. Adopt the constraint; reject the granularity calibrated to their unit (Scheppers' fronting rule, Marschall's syllable counts).
2. **Report convergence as architecture/feature convergence, never "their unit = our ATU."** The cross-corpus thesis is about the separating/binding *machine* and specific features recurring — not unit equivalence. Connects to [[feedback_check_prior_corpora]] (earn convergence, don't assume it) + [[feedback_staged_paper_scope_discipline]].
3. **Granularity mismatch is expected, not error.** When validating against an external witness, measure the direction that tests *our* boundaries (e.g. accent-oracle PRECISION: do our boundaries respect the accent hierarchy — yes, 98.5%), and refuse to report the other direction (RECALL: the te'amim disjoin far finer than idea-units) as "disagreement." A finer/coarser external unit producing extra/fewer cuts is the units being different objects.

Guards against the bandwagon reflex ([[feedback_rhetoric_bandwagon]]) — a scholar proposing a criterion is not a reason to adopt it; it is a reason to test it against the ATU bar.
