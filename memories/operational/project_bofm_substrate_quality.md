**Root cause of BoFM's recurring segmentation problems (diagnosed 2026-05-27):** the BoFM reader's ATU segmentation is built on a **Stanza UD auto-parse**, and Stanza is trained on MODERN English while the BoFM is **Early Modern English (EModE) biblical register** ("ye should suppose", "would that", "saying [quote]", stacked "that…that", "because ye are afflicted"). Stanza systematically mis-parses these → wrong clause boundaries → over-merges (Alma 32:24, 3 Ne 19, Mosiah 4:2) and over-splits. Unlike Tanakh (gold **BHSA** treebank) and GNT (gold **Macula** treebank), BoFM has **no gold treebank** — so the rules run on a noisy map. This is why the GNT ὅτι fix deployed cleanly (Macula gave reliable that-VP/sub-CL/role tags) and BoFM keeps needing patches.

**The trap:** we keep patching RULES (fabric guards, applier wiring) when the problem is the SUBSTRATE. Empirical proof (2026-05-27): wiring the canon appliers fixed ~700 violations but introduced ~54 new ones (subject-orphans, severed complements, frame breaks) — the appliers faithfully split where the noisy parse misled them. That +54 is the parse-quality ceiling made visible. **Stan: "we keep coming back to weaknesses/gaps in the bofm text fabric… how can we leverage NLP to make it more robust and similar to the bhsa?"** Per [[feedback_no_fly_swatting]] + [[feedback_check_prior_corpora]]: stop the rule whack-a-mole; invest in substrate quality.
